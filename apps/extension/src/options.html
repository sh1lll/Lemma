    Privacy

    • Lemma reads a page only when you ask it to (a right-click action, "Solve what's on screen", or the keyboard shortcut). It has no access to sites in the background and injects nothing until you invoke it.
    • Every calculation, solution, lesson and screen reading runs on this device. Page text, selections, screenshots and answers are never sent anywhere.
    • The only network request the extension can make is the one-time download of the English text-recognition data described above, if it was not bundled. It contains no information about you or the page.
    • Settings sync with your browser profile (storage.sync). Calculator history stays on this device (storage.local).
    • No analytics, no accounts, no ads.
