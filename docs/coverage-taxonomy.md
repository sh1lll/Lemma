# Lemma coverage taxonomy (owner's checklist)

The owner's goal: everything math or math-adjacent, from the most basic to rarely used, from every country's
curriculum, should be possible in Lemma. Every item below should either be **computed** (with readable steps or
results), **solved/drawn** (shapes, graphs), or, where it is purely conceptual (a proof, a definition, an open
problem), **answered honestly**: a short plain explanation, a worked example where one exists, and the assistant
offered for discussion. Never a confidently wrong answer.

Also required: **solving any shape** (any 2D/3D figure from whatever is given: sides, angles, radius, height,
diagonals, coordinates, area/volume back-solving), and math-adjacent fields: **physics, chemistry, statistics,
computer science, AI/ML, engineering, economics, business, medical**.

## 1. Arithmetic and number sense
Counting; natural, whole, integer, rational, irrational, real, complex numbers; positive/negative; absolute value;
opposites; number lines; place value; rounding; estimation; comparing and ordering; number properties.
Operations: + − × ÷, long division, order of operations (PEMDAS/BODMAS), mental arithmetic, algorithms, inverse ops.
Fractions: proper, improper, mixed, equivalent, simplifying, comparing, + − × ÷, complex fractions.
Decimals: notation, place value, terminating, repeating, fraction↔decimal, decimal arithmetic, significant figures.
Percentages: calculation, increase, decrease, change, percentage points, discounts, markups, tax, interest, reverse.
Number theory basics: factors, multiples, primes, composites, divisibility rules, prime factorization, GCD, LCM,
even/odd, perfect squares/cubes, exponents, roots, scientific notation, modular arithmetic.

## 2. Pre-algebra
Variables, constants, coefficients, expressions, equations, inequalities, notation, substitution, evaluating, like
terms, distributive/associative/commutative/identity/inverse properties, ratios, rates, unit rates, proportions,
direct and inverse variation, coordinate plane, ordered pairs, patterns, sequences, function intuition, word problems
→ equations.

## 3. Algebra
Elementary: expressions, linear, multi-step and literal equations, formulas, linear/compound inequalities, absolute
value equations/inequalities, systems of equations and inequalities (substitution, elimination, graphing), slope,
slope-intercept, point-slope, standard form, parallel/perpendicular lines.
Intermediate: polynomials (mono/bi/trinomials, + − × ÷), factoring (GCF, grouping, difference of squares, perfect
square trinomials, sum/difference of cubes), quadratics (formula, completing the square, inequalities,
discriminant), roots and zeros, rational expressions/equations/inequalities, radical expressions/equations, complex
and imaginary numbers, exponential and logarithmic equations.
Advanced (compute what is computable: groups Z_n / S_n / D_n orders, element orders, cosets, subgroups of small
groups, ring/field arithmetic mod p and GF(p^n), polynomial rings and ideals via Gröbner bases, field extension
degrees, minimal polynomials, Galois groups of small polynomials, homomorphism checks, Boolean algebra, Lie
brackets of matrices; explain the rest): abstract, linear, Boolean, commutative, associative, Lie, universal algebra,
groups, rings, fields, modules, vector spaces, algebras over fields, homo/iso/automorphisms, quotients, polynomial
rings, ideals, field extensions, Galois theory, representation theory.

## 4. Geometry
Basic: points, lines, segments, rays, planes, angles (acute, obtuse, right, straight, complementary, supplementary,
vertical, adjacent), parallel/perpendicular lines, transversals, angle relationships.
Plane: triangles (equilateral, isosceles, scalene, right), triangle inequality, congruence, similarity, Pythagoras,
quadrilaterals (square, rectangle, rhombus, parallelogram, trapezoid, kite), polygons, regular polygons, interior/
exterior angles, diagonals, perimeter, area.
Circles: radius, diameter, circumference, chords, arcs, sectors, tangents, secants, central/inscribed angles, arc
length, sector area, circle theorems, cyclic quadrilaterals, power of a point.
Solid: cubes, rectangular prisms, cylinders, cones, spheres, pyramids, prisms, polyhedra, Platonic solids, surface
area, volume, cross-sections, nets, Euler's formula. (Also frustums, tori, hemispheres, ellipsoids, capsules, wedges,
spherical caps, composite solids.)
Coordinate: Cartesian, distance, midpoint, slope, lines, circles, conics (parabolas, ellipses, hyperbolas), loci,
transformations.
Advanced (compute where possible: hyperbolic distance/area, spherical triangles, curvature of curves/surfaces,
geodesics on simple surfaces, convex hulls, polygon area from coordinates, fractal dimension of standard fractals,
projective cross-ratio, inversion; explain the rest): Euclidean, non-Euclidean, hyperbolic, elliptic, projective,
affine, differential, Riemannian, algebraic, discrete, convex, computational, symplectic, contact, fractal,
incidence, synthetic, inversive geometry.

## 5. Trigonometry
Degrees, radians, unit circle, reference and coterminal angles, arc length, angular velocity, sin/cos/tan/csc/sec/
cot, SOH-CAH-TOA. Identities: Pythagorean, reciprocal, quotient, even/odd, cofunction, sum/difference, double/half
angle, product-to-sum, sum-to-product. Applications: laws of sines and cosines, solving triangles (incl. ambiguous
SSA), inverse trig, trig equations and inequalities, sinusoids (amplitude, period, phase, vertical shift), harmonic
motion, polar coordinates. Advanced: spherical trigonometry, hyperbolic functions, Fourier series, complex trig.

## 6. Functions and relations
Relations, functions, domain, codomain, range, notation, independent/dependent variables, evaluation, piecewise,
composite, inverse, one-to-one/onto/bijective, even/odd, periodic, increasing/decreasing, bounded, continuous/
discontinuous, transformations, composition, families (linear, quadratic, polynomial, rational, radical, absolute
value, exponential, logarithmic, trig, inverse trig, step, greatest integer, logistic, hyperbolic, special functions).

## 7. Sequences, series, patterns
Arithmetic, geometric, harmonic, Fibonacci, recursive; explicit/recursive formulas; finite/infinite sequences;
arithmetic/geometric/infinite series; convergence/divergence; power, Taylor, Maclaurin, alternating, harmonic,
telescoping, binomial, Fourier series; sequence limits; tests: ratio, root, integral, comparison, alternating.

## 8. Calculus
Differential: limits (one-sided, infinite, at infinity), continuity, IVT, derivatives, difference quotients,
differentiability, power/product/quotient/chain rules, implicit and logarithmic differentiation, higher order,
related rates, linear approximation, differentials, MVT, Rolle, L'Hôpital, critical points, local/absolute extrema,
increasing/decreasing, concavity, inflection points, curve sketching, optimization.
Integral: antiderivatives, indefinite/definite integrals, Riemann sums, FTC, substitution, parts, partial fractions,
trig substitution, improper, numerical integration, area under/between curves, volumes of revolution (disk, washer,
shell), arc length, surface area, average value, work, fluid pressure, accumulation functions.
Multivariable: limits, partial derivatives (higher order), gradient, directional derivatives, tangent planes, chain
rule, Jacobian, Hessian, double/triple/iterated integrals, change of variables, Lagrange multipliers, vector fields,
line/surface/volume integrals, divergence, curl, flux, Green, Stokes, divergence theorem.
Advanced: real analysis, measure theory, integration theory, functional analysis, calculus of variations
(Euler–Lagrange), distributions, fractional calculus, nonstandard analysis.

## 9. Linear algebra
Scalars, vectors, addition, scalar multiplication, vector spaces, subspaces, linear combinations, span, (in)dependence,
basis, dimension, matrices (+, ×, transpose, inverse), determinants, rank, row reduction, Gaussian / Gauss–Jordan,
linear systems, null/column/row spaces, eigenvalues/vectors, characteristic polynomial, diagonalization,
orthogonality, projections, Gram–Schmidt, QR, LU, SVD, positive definite, symmetric, Hermitian, factorizations,
linear transformations, change of basis, inner product spaces, spectral theorem, tensors.

## 10. Probability
Experiments, outcomes, sample spaces, events, axioms, complements, mutually exclusive/independent events,
conditional/joint/marginal probability, multiplication/addition rules, Bayes, total probability.
Combinatorics: counting principle, permutations, combinations, factorials, binomial and multinomial coefficients,
inclusion–exclusion, pigeonhole, generating functions, recurrences.
Distributions: uniform, Bernoulli, binomial, geometric, negative binomial, hypergeometric, Poisson, normal, standard
normal, exponential, gamma, beta, chi-square, t, F, Weibull, lognormal, multinomial, categorical, Pareto, Cauchy,
logistic, Gumbel, GEV.
Advanced: random variables, PDF/PMF/CDF, expectation, variance, SD, covariance, correlation, MGFs, characteristic
functions, LLN, CLT, Markov chains (stationary distributions, absorption), stochastic processes, Brownian motion,
martingales, stochastic calculus/Itô, Monte Carlo, Bayesian probability, measure-theoretic probability.

## 11. Statistics
Descriptive: mean, median, mode, range, variance, SD, quartiles, percentiles, IQR, five-number summary, frequency
tables, histograms, box plots, scatterplots, skewness, kurtosis, outliers.
Inferential: populations, samples, sampling methods/distributions/variability, standard errors, CIs, margin of error,
hypothesis tests (null/alternative, p-values, significance, Type I/II, power), one-/two-sample, paired, z, t,
chi-square, F, ANOVA.
Regression: Pearson, Spearman, simple/multiple/logistic/polynomial/nonlinear regression, residuals, least squares,
R², inference, multicollinearity (VIF), heteroscedasticity, autocorrelation (Durbin–Watson), diagnostics.
Advanced: Bayesian, frequentist, nonparametric (Mann–Whitney, Wilcoxon, Kruskal–Wallis), multivariate, robust,
time series (AR/MA/ARIMA, smoothing), survival (Kaplan–Meier), causal inference, design of experiments, factor
analysis, PCA, clustering (k-means), discriminant analysis, bootstrap, jackknife, meta-analysis, missing data,
statistical learning.

## 12–14. Discrete math, number theory, logic and set theory
Propositional/predicate logic, truth tables, quantifiers, equivalence, implications, proof techniques (explain +
check small cases, verify induction steps symbolically where possible), sets and operations, relations (equivalence,
partial orders), functions, counting, graph theory, trees, Boolean algebra, recurrences, discrete probability.
Number theory: divisibility, primes, factorization, Euclidean algorithm (with steps), congruences, residues, Fermat,
Euler, CRT, Wilson, Diophantine (linear, Pell), quadratic residues (Legendre/Jacobi), primitive roots, continued
fractions, perfect/Mersenne/Fermat primes, prime counting π(x) and the PNT estimate, zeta values, computational NT.
Logic/sets: first-order logic, formal systems, axioms, proof/model/computability theory, ZF(C), choice,
cardinality, countability, ordinals/cardinals, transfinite arithmetic (ℵ₀ + 1, ω + 1 vs 1 + ω), Gödel, continuum
hypothesis, type theory, constructive math → concise explanations and examples.

## 15. Differential equations
ODEs: first/second/higher order, linear/nonlinear, separable, exact, homogeneous, Bernoulli, IVPs, BVPs, systems,
autonomous systems, equilibria, stability (Jacobian eigenvalues). PDEs: heat, wave, Laplace, Poisson, Schrödinger,
Navier–Stokes, Maxwell, diffusion, transport, boundary/initial conditions, separation of variables, Fourier methods,
finite differences/elements (worked small examples). Dynamical systems, chaos (logistic map, Lyapunov exponent),
bifurcation, stability, perturbation, numerical ODEs (Euler, RK4 with steps), SDEs, fractional DEs.

## 16. Optimization and operations research
Linear/integer/mixed-integer/nonlinear/convex/quadratic/dynamic programming, combinatorial and constrained
optimization, gradient descent/SGD, Newton's method, Lagrange, KKT, Pareto/multiobjective, network optimization,
transportation, assignment (Hungarian), scheduling, routing (TSP small), queueing (M/M/1, M/M/c), inventory (EOQ),
decision analysis (expected value, decision trees), game theory (Nash equilibria of small games, minimax), optimal
control basics.

## 17. Graph theory and networks
Directed/undirected/weighted/complete/bipartite/connected graphs, cycles, paths, walks, trees, spanning trees, MST
(Kruskal/Prim), shortest paths (Dijkstra/Bellman–Ford), coloring/chromatic number, degree, adjacency/incidence
matrices, max flow/min cut, matching, planarity, Euler/Hamilton paths, random graphs, spectral graph theory,
centrality, social/complex networks. Input as edge lists ("A-B 3, B-C 2…") or adjacency matrices.

## 18–19. Modeling, numerical and computational mathematics
Model formulation, parameters, constraints, deterministic/stochastic, discrete/continuous, linear/nonlinear,
simulation, sensitivity, calibration, parameter estimation, validation, error analysis, dimensional analysis,
scaling laws, compartmental (SIR/SEIR), agent-based, population (logistic, Lotka–Volterra), epidemiological,
economic, physical, financial models. Numerical: floating point (IEEE 754 representation, machine epsilon), error
propagation, stability, numerical differentiation/integration (trapezoid, Simpson), root finding (bisection, Newton,
secant — with iteration tables), interpolation (Lagrange, Newton, splines), extrapolation, numerical linear algebra,
finite differences/elements, Monte Carlo, RNG, complexity (Big-O), approximation theory.

## 20. Advanced and specialized
Analysis (real, complex — residues, contour integrals, Laurent series; functional, harmonic, Fourier, measure,
Lebesgue, operator, spectral, asymptotic), topology (Euler characteristic, genus, knot invariants for small knots,
metric space checks; explain the rest), algebra (group/ring/field/Galois/module/representation/Lie/homological/
category theory), geometry branches, information theory (entropy, KL, mutual information, channel capacity), coding
theory (Hamming codes, parity, CRC, checksums), cryptography (Caesar/Vigenère, RSA toy examples, modular
exponentiation, Diffie–Hellman small numbers, hashing explained), quantum math (qubit states, Pauli matrices, tensor
products, Born rule), fuzzy logic, fractals, chaos, game theory, ergodic theory, Ramsey/extremal/additive/algebraic
combinatorics.

## 21. Cross-cutting concepts
Symmetry, proportion, scaling, invariance, transformation, optimization, convergence/divergence, recursion,
iteration, approximation, interpolation/extrapolation, randomness, dimensionality, continuity/discreteness,
linearity/nonlinearity, equilibrium, stability, causality, duality, recurrence, asymptotics, universality, entropy,
feedback, emergence, conservation, propagation, diffusion, random walks, path dependence. (Answer "what is X" with a
short explanation and a computable example where one exists.)

## Quantitative disciplines (applied)
- **Quantitative foundations:** units, orders of magnitude, error propagation, dimensional analysis, normalization.
- **Econometrics/economics:** panel/fixed/random effects, IV, RDD, DiD, ARIMA/VAR/GARCH, cointegration, Granger,
  Kalman; elasticity, consumer/producer surplus, supply–demand equilibrium, utility maximization, production
  functions, GDP/inflation/real vs nominal, multiplier, Gini, game-theoretic economics, auctions.
- **Quantitative finance:** TVM, PV/FV, annuities, perpetuities, IRR/NPV, yields, duration, convexity, forward/swap
  rates, no-arbitrage, portfolio theory (Markowitz, efficient frontier, CAPM, Sharpe, Kelly, risk parity), derivatives
  (put-call parity, Black–Scholes, binomial trees, Greeks, implied vol, American/barrier/Asian/digital options),
  risk (VaR, CVaR/ES, drawdown, credit PD/LGD/EAD), trading math (expectancy, position sizing, slippage, Sharpe/
  Sortino/Calmar, backtest stats), macro (yield curves, inflation).
- **Computational math/scientific computing, data science/ML:** numerical methods, simulation, automatic
  differentiation, EDA, normalization/standardization, metrics (accuracy, precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, confusion
  matrix, log loss, MSE/MAE/RMSE), gradient descent steps, softmax, sigmoid, cross-entropy, regularization,
  backpropagation on small nets, attention/transformer arithmetic (parameter counts, FLOPs), k-means, kNN, decision
  tree split gain (entropy/Gini), naive Bayes, SVM margins, PCA.
- **Operations research/decision math:** as in §16, plus MDPs, multi-criteria decisions.
- **Information theory & signal processing:** entropy, KL, cross-entropy, channel capacity, coding, Fourier/DFT/FFT,
  convolution, filters, sampling theorem/Nyquist, aliasing, SNR/decibels, power spectral density.
- **Physics:** mechanics (kinematics, Newton, energy, momentum, rotation, gravitation, orbits), Lagrangian/
  Hamiltonian, fluids, thermodynamics, statistical mechanics, electromagnetism, circuits, waves, optics, acoustics,
  relativity (time dilation, E=mc², Lorentz), quantum (de Broglie, photon energy, particle in a box, uncertainty),
  nuclear (half-life, decay), astronomy.
- **Chemistry:** moles, molar mass from formulas, stoichiometry, limiting reagent, percent yield, concentration/
  dilution, gas laws, pH/pOH/Ka/Kb/buffers (Henderson–Hasselbalch), equilibrium constants, kinetics (rate laws,
  half-life, Arrhenius), thermochemistry (Hess, ΔG = ΔH − TΔS), electrochemistry (Nernst), balancing equations,
  empirical/molecular formulas, isotopes.
- **Engineering:** control (transfer functions, poles, stability, PID), circuits (Ohm, Kirchhoff, RC/RL/RLC,
  impedance, power), structures (stress, strain, beams, moments of inertia), fluids (Reynolds, Bernoulli, flow),

- **Computer science:** binary/hex/octal/two's complement, bitwise ops, Boolean logic, Big-O, data sizes (KiB vs KB),
  bandwidth/transfer time, hashing, IEEE floats, recursion counts, graph algorithms, number bases up to 36, ASCII/
  Unicode codes, checksums, CIDR/subnets.
- **AI/ML:** as above; also model sizes (parameters → memory at fp16/int8), token/compute estimates, learning-rate

- **Biology/medical:** BMI, BMR, BSA, dosage by weight, IV drip rates, dilutions, creatinine clearance, half-life/

  (R₀, SIR), population genetics (Hardy–Weinberg), growth models, Michaelis–Menten.
- **Social sciences:** psychometrics (z-scores, reliability/Cronbach's α), demography, voting methods (plurality,
  ranked choice, Borda, Condorcet), apportionment, survey margin of error, network measures.
- **Business:** margins, markup, break-even, ROI, CAC/LTV, churn, cohort retention, NPS, pricing/elasticity,
